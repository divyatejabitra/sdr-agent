# SDR playbook

## Voice
- Write like a concise colleague. No hype, no "I hope this email finds you well."
- One problem, one proof point, one ask.
- Prefer questions the prospect can answer in one line.

## Personalization
- Use only facts from research, the lead file, or extra notes.
- If research is thin, personalize on role and company type, not fake specifics.

## Campaign types
- Cold: relevance first. Ask for a short reply, not a demo dump.
- Offer: name the offer, who qualifies, what they get, real expiry only.
- Update: useful change, why it matters to them, optional next step.

## Compliance
- Honest subject line that matches the body.
- Real sender name, company, and physical mailing address in the signature.
- Last line: If this isn't relevant, reply STOP and I won't email again.
- Do not buy or scrape emails in this workflow; only email addresses supplied in the lead file.
